# BOM notes

Prices are indicative concept estimates (TRL 2) and will be confirmed with named suppliers at TRL 3. Item numbers match the exploded view (`media/exploded.png`) and the components table in SWC-PRC-001; items 12 and 13 are not modelled.

| Group | Items | Indicative cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One pack | 1 to 7, 12, 13 | about $370 (cells about $117) |
| One wall dock | 8 to 11 | about $145 |
| Total | 1 to 13 | about $515 |

One pack and one dock fit within the $700 concept budget in `project.yaml`. The remaining margin of about $185 is meant for fire-safe containment for building and charging, access to a spot welder, and spare cells. A second pack, which a real swap demonstration would need, adds about $370 and would take the total past the budget; see `docs/REVIEW.md` (proposed, awaiting Amish).

Cells must be new, matched and bought from an authorized distributor. Do not substitute recycled or unknown-grade cells in the reference pack.
